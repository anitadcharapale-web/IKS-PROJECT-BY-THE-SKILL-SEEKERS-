# Gunas and the Big Five

A single-page explainer comparing the three **gunas** (sattva, rajas, tamas) from Indian philosophy with the **Big Five** personality model, and sketching where the two might meet.

The page covers:

- Where the research stands
- What the gunas are
- The Big Five
- Where the two models might meet
- What has been studied, and what has not
- Why India needs a careful comparison
- A study that would fill the gap
- Limits and sources

## View it

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step, no dependencies (fonts load from Google Fonts and fall back to system fonts offline).

**Live version (GitHub Pages):** after pushing, go to *Settings → Pages*, choose *Deploy from a branch*, pick `main` and `/ (root)`. The site will appear at `https://<your-username>.github.io/gunas-and-the-big-five/`.
